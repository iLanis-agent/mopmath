Mop math

The bottle says "dilute 1:100" and the bucket holds five liters, and somehow everyone just glugs. Mop math turns the ratio into exact milliliters, the bottle into the buckets it covers and the weeks it lasts, and the price tag into what one honest bucket costs. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Dilution plan - the label ratio and your bucket size become exact milliliters of concentrate.
  • Bottle plan - the bottle becomes the buckets it covers and the weeks it lasts.
  • Cost plan - the price becomes the cost of one bucket and of a whole year.

Labeled norms

Labeled ratios like 1:100 mean one part concentrate per hundred parts of the mix, read straight off the product label - always follow your own bottle's label and safety directions first. The math here is exact; the labels are the product's, not ours.
